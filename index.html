PART 4. SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION – COMPARISONS WITH NOUNS (40 POINTS)

  1. My new school has 30 classrooms. My old school has 20 classrooms. (more … than)
    → My new school has
  2. Sarah has 5 close friends. Emily also has 5 close friends. (as many … as)
    → Sarah has
  3. Tom has 3 assignments this week. Peter has 6 assignments. (fewer … than)
    → Tom has
  4. This university offers 20 scholarships. That university offers 10 scholarships. (twice as many … as)
    → This university offers
  5. Jack spends 2 hours studying every day. His brother spends 4 hours studying every day. (half as much … as)
    → Jack spends
